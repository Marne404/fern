Meet your scout

Dress up.
Head out.

A round little hiker with noodle arms, a merit-badge sash and a backpack that is always a bit too big. Pick colors, a hat and a face. They bounce, flail, wave, laugh and facepalm along with you – and they live in the world: catching snowflakes, holding on to the hat in a gust, pointing at shooting stars, warming their hands at the fire. Whatever is in the backpack hangs on it, and you can take things in your hands: a walking stick, a swinging lantern, the trail map, a harmonica.

It's the same editor as in the game. Hold G (or LB on a controller) on the trail for the emote wheel with 32 emotes, and V switches to third person.

The trail

Twenty-two moods,
one endless path

Autumn meadows melt into wheat fields, cherry valleys, white birch woods, forests of giant pines, mushroom woods, rock gorges and heather moors under northern lights. Every biome has its own corners – a birch stand here, a fern hollow there – and the next one grows in slowly, grove by grove. Every world grows from a seed, so type in a word and your friends set off on the very same trail – which biome comes next then depends a little on your pace and the hour.

A living day

Mist at dawn,
fireflies at dusk

The sun travels over every valley. Mornings begin in golden mist, evenings glow, and short moonlit nights sparkle with stars and fireflies. And every biome tends to arrive at its finest hour: the cherry valley at dawn, the golden birch slopes in the golden hour, the glowing woods at night, the deadwood bog in the rain – the clock even slows a little to let the moment last. Or pick Each biome at its best and the hour simply follows the trail.

Out there

Stop for a while

The trail is full of reasons to linger: skies that change with the hour, snow and fog and far-off storms, campfires and shelters, ancient trees, berries to pick, cairns to build – and animals that trust you if you sit still.

Obstacles

The world poses problems.
You find the answers.

There is no intended solution, only physics, your stamina and whatever is in your backpack.

1

The fallen tree

Long before you see it, the banks close in and turn to rock. Then a giant trunk lies across the lane and there is no way around it.

  • Crouch underneathunless your pack weighs more than 12 kg, then you get stuck
  • Climb overcosts stamina; over 16 kg of luggage it's simply impossible
2

The broken bridge

A wide river, a strong current and the stumps of what used to be a bridge.

  • Push a log across and balanceyour load acts as a lever, so lean the wrong way and you're swimming
  • Swimthe current drifts you downstream and everything in your pack gets wet
  • Knot two ropes, tie them to the posts and climb acrossa sloppy knot plus a heavy pack slowly comes loose
3

The cliff

After a long climb the trail stops at a 20 meter drop with a waterfall pool far below.

  • Jump into the poolthe water breaks your fall, if you hit it
  • Rappel downwith rope and a good knot
  • Take the long detoura side ramp winds down, slowly
4

The pasture fence

Climb the stile, or go looking for the end of the fence.

5

The mud hollow

Wading costs time, stamina and dry boots. The stepping stones cost courage.

6

The rockslide

Boulders as big as houses. Find the winding gap, or climb.

Field guide

Fifty-five things worth carrying

Cheese and cocoa, a compass that points to the next obstacle, a lantern for the dusk, a kite for windy hills, a harmonica for the long evenings. Everything weighs something, and your body keeps count.

Merit badges

What makes Fern, Fern

Listen to your body

No health bars. Hunger, thirst, cold, wet clothes and a heavy pack show in how you walk, breathe and see.

Everything has weight

Pack light and you're quick. Pack for everything and every climb hurts. Fifty-five items, all with a purpose.

Endless & seeded

The trail never ends. Type any word as a seed and your friends start in exactly the same world.

Weather that moves

Gusts chase cloud shadows over the hills, showers leave puddles and rainbows, dust devils spin through the desert.

A song for every step

56 tracks shift with the biome and the moment: calm when you rest, tense on the rope, splashy in the river.

Runs on modest PCs

Low to Extreme presets, distant trees as impostors, every optimization can be toggled. From a handheld to a gaming rig.

Say it with a wave

An emote wheel full of gestures: point the way, cheer at the summit, facepalm at the river.

Ready to talk

Voice chat is on its way: push to talk, always on or voice activation already work, and your scout's mouth moves as you speak.
