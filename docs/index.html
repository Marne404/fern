Fern

An endless hike through painted worlds
⬇ Windows ⬇ Linux Screenshots
Free · no installation · unzip and play

Walk. Wonder. Keep going.

Fern is a game about the journey, not the destination. The trail never ends and your distance is your score. Most of the time you simply walk and take in the landscape – until the world puts a real, physical problem in your way, and there is no scripted solution.

🌍

Endless world

Every world grows from a seed. Type any word, share it with friends, and you'll walk the very same trail.

🍂

12 biomes

Autumn meadows, blossom groves, mountain pines, sunset coasts, glowing forests, endless deserts and more.

🌬️

Living weather

Gusts roll through the grass, leaves drift from the trees, tumbleweeds bounce and dust devils wander by.

🎒

Backpack & body

Hunger, thirst, cold and a heavy backpack wear you down. Swim with your camera and it's gone.

🪢

Ropes & knots

Tie ropes in a little knot minigame. A sloppy knot might hold… or give way halfway across the river.

🎵

Dynamic music

56 tracks: every biome has its own playlist, tense music near obstacles and quiet tunes while resting.

No scripted solutions

Obstacles are made of systems – weight, physics, stamina, water and knot quality. The same river plays out differently every time.

The broken bridge

  • Push a log across and balance over it
  • Swim through the current – and soak your backpack
  • Knot two ropes, stretch them between the posts, climb across

The fallen giant

  • A trunk wedged across a narrowing gorge
  • Crouch underneath – not with a heavy backpack!
  • Or spend your stamina climbing over it

The waterfall cliff

  • After a long climb, a 20 m drop
  • Jump into the pool – the water breaks your fall
  • Rappel down on a rope, or take the long way round

⬇ Windows ⬇ Linux

Unzip and run Fern.exe (Windows) or Fern.x86_64 (Linux). Windows may warn about an unknown publisher – click More info → Run anyway.

Runs on integrated graphics on the Medium preset; High and Ultra (real grass blades, film look, soft shadows) want a proper graphics card.
